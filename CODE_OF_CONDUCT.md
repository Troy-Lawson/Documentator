# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in this
project's issues, pull requests, discussions, or other community spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate confidentiality.
Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict
participation when necessary to maintain a safe, productive project.
